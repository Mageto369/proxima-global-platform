SMACHS Foundation
Founded by Charlene Ruto, daughter of President William Ruto (Kenya). SMACHS focuses on smallholder farmer empowerment and rural livelihoods across Central Kenya, Rift Valley, and Coast regions. Proxima Global provides the core agri-tech hardware and software infrastructure; SMACHS handles community integration and farmer training.
